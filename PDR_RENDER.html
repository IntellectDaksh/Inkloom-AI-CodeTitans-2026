Executive Summary

Inkloom AI is an automated, pre-publication brand governance firewall and marketing claim verification platform. It systematically intercepts marketing copy across social channels, paid ad variants, landing pages, and email campaigns before publication. By verifying assertions against registered clinical trials, ISO laboratory standards, statutory registers, and brand voice guidelines, Inkloom prevents multimillion-dollar regulatory fines and brand liabilities while keeping human reviewers in complete control.

Problem Statement

Generative AI tools allow marketing teams to produce thousands of digital assets each week at near-zero marginal cost, drastically magnifying enterprise liability:

• Unsubstantiated Claims: Overpromising phrases ("cures illness", "2x faster in 14 days") slip into ad copy without randomized double-blind placebo control trials (CARSE standard).
• Statutory Omissions: Campaigns routinely violate mandatory statutory notices (FTC 16 CFR § 255 endorsement disclosures, FDA DSHEA § 403(r)(6) dietary disclaimers).
• Regulatory Drift: Approved ads remain in digital circulation long after the underlying clinical trial, patent certification, or ISO license has expired.
• Fragmented Workflows: Enterprise legal review relies on static PDFs, dormant spreadsheets, and informal Slack sign-offs with zero auditable provenance.

Proposed Solution

Inkloom AI establishes an end-to-end deterministic and AI-assisted governance firewall:

• Deterministic Pre-Flight (<5ms): Regex scanner catches banned superlatives and missing statutory notices before invoking expensive LLMs.
• Evidence-Grounded AI Analysis: Google Gemini Flash evaluates context against active brand claims; ungrounded statements are strictly flagged with zero hallucinated sources.
• Word-Level Surgical Diffing: Preserves brand voice by highlighting exact non-compliant tokens and offering 1-click clinically substantiated replacements.
• Living Claims & Cascade Engine: Expiration dates trigger automated cascade re-reviews across all historically approved campaign drafts.

Brand Managers Multi-brand scoping, guidelines & risk analytics.

Figure 2.1: Architectural Topology — 8-Stage Governance Lifecycle, Dual-Tier Database Persistence, and Deterministic Pre-Flight Guardrails.
1. Deterministic-First Pre-Flight

Running regex rule checks prior to LLM calls eliminates 80% of common violations in under 5ms. It deterministically blocks banned superlatives ("miracle", "unhackable") and statutory disclosure omissions (FTC #ad, FDA DSHEA notices), protecting API quotas and lowering P95 latency to 420ms.

2. Multi-Tenant Database Isolation

Enforced via PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) across 25 schema tables. Claims, evidence assets, and guidelines are partitioned strictly by brand_id. Vector embeddings (1536 dim) assert complete isolation, ensuring zero cross-brand evidence leakage between Apex Nutrition, Lumina Skincare, and Zenith Cloud.

3. Dual-Tier Persistence & Ledger

Pairs a production 25-table PostgreSQL migration script with an embedded transactional engine (InkloomStore). This guarantees zero cold-start pauses during evaluation. All preflights, edits, disputes, and approvals emit immutable SHA-256 state hashes into audit_events satisfying FDA 21 CFR Part 11.

Architecture Reconciliation Note: The official Round 2 submission text describes the analytical core as a 6-stage AI governance pipeline (Segmentation, Regex, Retrieval, LLM Analysis, Finding Generation, Scoring/Diffing). The live production application visualizes this as an 8-stage governance lifecycle by explicitly presenting initial ingestion segmentation and the terminal cryptographic audit logging & cascade re-review trigger. Both descriptions reflect the same unified codebase.

Human-in-the-Loop Multi-Action Governance Gate

Reviewer Recorded Override: Authorized compliance officers (reviewer / admin roles) can record formal legal overrides, signing with their digital ID and rationale.
